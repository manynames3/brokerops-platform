# Paid Pilot Plan

## First customer niche

Independent insurance agencies and MGAs that reconcile monthly carrier commission statements manually in spreadsheets.

## Buyer

Operations, finance, or agency principal responsible for commission accuracy, carrier follow-up, and audit evidence.

## Pilot offer

- Starting price: $750/month after setup
- Scope: one agency or MGA, one carrier statement workflow, one policy/account export, one operator team
- Duration: 30-60 days
- Deliverable: imported statement history, exception queue, evidence-grounded AI review notes, human review status, and CSV exception report

## Qualification criteria

- Buyer can provide sanitized or approved production policy/account export.
- Buyer can provide one recurring carrier statement CSV.
- Buyer has measurable reconciliation pain: time spent, missed commissions, audit exposure, or delayed month-end close.
- Buyer accepts that the first pilot is focused on one workflow, not broad carrier automation.

## Paid-only surface

- Production customer imports
- Persistent reconciliation history
- AI-assisted exception review
- Human review workflow
- Audit trail
- Exception report export
- Team access

## Not included yet

- Self-serve billing
- Enterprise SSO
- Broad carrier template library
- SOC 2 claims
- Automated carrier portal scraping

## Success criteria

- At least one real statement imported successfully.
- Exceptions are detected and reviewed.
- Buyer can export an exception report without founder assistance.
- Buyer can name a measurable workflow improvement before renewal.
